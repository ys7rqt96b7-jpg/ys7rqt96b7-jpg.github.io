Privacy Policy

MMY Publishing uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms). Google's Privacy Policy: http://www.google.com/policies/privacy.

What it accesses

Only the owner's own YouTube channel, Facebook Page and Instagram account, through the permissions the owner grants (upload and manage his own videos, captions and playlists, and post to his own Page and account). It reads back the settings of the videos it uploads, to check them.

What it stores

On the owner's computer only, the sign-in credentials that keep it connected and a record of each upload (the video's ID and the settings sent and read back). It does not access, collect, store or share any other person's data, uses no cookies, and sells or shares nothing with third parties.

How to revoke access

At Google's security settings page (https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions), choose MMY Publishing and remove its access; on Meta, in Business Settings, revoke the system user's tokens. When access is revoked, the stored credentials and records are deleted within 7 days.
